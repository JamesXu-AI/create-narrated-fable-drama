## Mission and hard scope

Build only children's educational stories whose primary dramatic world is a
forest community of non-human animals. Do not preserve generic cross-genre
support. Reject or return a task before story generation when its main story is
about humans, domestic life, cities, farms, oceans, vehicles, science fiction, or
another non-forest subject.

A brief human storyteller/listener bookend is allowed only when the supplied
source requires it. It must remain a framing device around the forest-animal
teaching story, never replace the forest as the main dramatic world.

Every accepted `task.json` must declare exactly:

```json
{
  "story_domain_profile": "forest_animal_education",
  "visual_style_profile": "soft_cute_3d_healing_animation"
}
```

These are project identity, not optional preferences. A missing or different
value blocks every department.

## Educational story and animal authority

- Demonstrate one age-appropriate educational principle through animal choices,
  cause, consequence, changed understanding, and a complete ending. Do not rely
  on a detached lecture or generic closing slogan.
- Keep the forest community and its social/ecological relationships legible.
  Distinguish individuals and silent groups by species, scale, behavior, route,
  and story function.
- Lock each recurring animal's species, body topology, locomotion, relative scale,
  coat/fur/feather/skin material, color pattern, face, ears/horns/beak/tail/wings,
  clothing, accessories, and temporary physical states.
- Preserve natural species anatomy and feasible locomotion. Expressive faces,
  gaze, speech, and story-motivated accessories are allowed; added human torsos,
  arms, hands, feet, or upright bipedal anatomy are not.
- Do not swap one animal species for another, change predator/prey scale, invent
  extra limbs, duplicate a performer, or turn an approved animal into a generic
  lookalike between assets, Shots, Segments, or revisions.
- A storybook forest may contain a fable community broader than one real-world
  habitat when the source requires it. Do not silently replace that authored
  roster in the name of ecological realism. When the lesson itself concerns
  ecology or species facts, factual habitat and behavior take precedence.

## Forest-world continuity authority

Production design owns one coherent forest-world bible for each task and encodes
it in the production-design plan, reusable asset catalog, and final Location
masters. Do not create a second world-bible or location-continuity copy.

For every forest Location, lock:

1. forest type and the relationship among canopy, understory, forest floor,
   clearing, trail, den/nest, rock, roots, and water where present;
2. season, weather, time of day, light direction, shadow softness, moisture, and
   atmosphere;
3. stable vegetation species language, bark/leaf/soil/stone/water materials, and
   the approved warm low-saturation palette;
4. zones, connections, entrances, exits, walkable or flyable routes, fixed
   obstacles, interaction points, and relative scale;
5. persistent landmarks and their screen direction, distance, orientation, and
   visibility requirements;
6. fixed set dressing, embedded animal population, independent performers, nests,
   dens, food, tools, and mutable props;
7. the continuous forest ambience: wind, leaves, insects, birds, water, and
   distance, including every authored change.

Reuse the same approved Location master for every unchanged revisit. A closer crop,
different camera angle, or missing offscreen landmark never authorizes a new
forest layout. Create a new Location or state only for an authored move, seasonal
change, weather/time transition, damage, construction, or other visible story
change, and propagate that change through every dependent artifact.

Within a Location chain, preserve animal presence, entrances/exits, tracks,
injuries, carried objects, prop placement, vegetation disturbance, weather,
lighting phase, and ambient sound. Never teleport an animal or prop, reverse a
trail or landmark without motivation, regrow damaged foliage, dry wet ground, or
reset time/weather between adjacent Segments.

Apply the separate
[Soft & Cute 3D Healing Animation Visual Standard](../direct-production-design/references/soft-cute-3d-healing-visual-standard.md)
to the complete forest world and every animal asset.

## Department obligations

- **`screenplay-writer`:** accept only the fixed project domain; make the forest
  geography, animal entrances/exits, species identity, educational cause, and
  resulting state explicit without choosing final visual design.
- **`direct-production-design`:** author the one forest-world authority using
  dressed Location masters and existing continuity fields; lock animal identity,
  relative scale, materials, vegetation, topology, landmarks, weather, light, and
  population.
- **`previsualize-cinematography`:** inherit the exact Location and animal
  authorities; preserve screen direction, geography, routes, population, state,
  light, atmosphere, and forest ambience in every Shot and revisit.
- **`virtual-production`:** bind the approved Location master in every forest
  Segment, bind each independent animal authority, and use predecessor evidence
  only for temporal state. Never let the provider invent or redesign the forest.
- **`seedance-video-review`:** reject forest-layout drift, species/scale/anatomy
  drift, population loss or duplication, landmark reversal, prop teleportation,
  vegetation/weather/light resets, and ambience discontinuity.
- **`finish-postproduction`:** preserve forest palette, material response,
  geography, time/weather progression, environmental sound, and animal identity;
  do not conceal a continuity failure with grading, reframing, transitions, or
  sound replacement.

## Release gates

Before releasing any department, verify:

1. both fixed task profiles are present and exact;
2. the educational lesson is caused and demonstrated by the forest-animal story;
3. every recurring animal matches its approved species, topology, relative scale,
   markings, material, and state;
4. every forest revisit matches its Location master, topology, landmarks,
   population, vegetation, palette, season, weather, time, light, and ambience;
5. every adjacent Segment carries animal, prop, environment, and sound state
   without unexplained reset;
6. every authored forest change is visible, causal, and propagated downstream.

Any failure blocks release and returns to the department that owns the incorrect
authority. Do not make a generic fallback.
